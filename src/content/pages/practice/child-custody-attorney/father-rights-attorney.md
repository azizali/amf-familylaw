## Chicago Firm Protects Fathers’ Rights

![Father-with-child](../../../../assets/legacy/7-father-with-child.jpg)A common misconception is that fathers have limited rights regarding their impact on their children. As a father you have the right to seek primary decision-making (formerly custody) and parenting time (formerly visitation) with your child.

The Law Offices of Annette M. Fernholz, P.C. has represented many fathers in parenting disputes. Several fathers have been granted primary placement of their children by agreement or after lengthy litigation. Other clients enjoy shared parenting time where they have about 50% of the  time with their children.

A change in your circumstances, your ex-partner’s or your child’s might be a basis to modify an existing parenting order.  Your former partner’s change in employment, location or family unit may adversely impact your parenting time, which would warrant a modification to an existing court order. If you are denied court-ordered parenting time, you may proceed on visitation abuse charges.
